# Privacy Policy for VBTV video controls

**Last Updated:** August 30, 2026

## Overview

This Chrome extension ("VBTV video controls") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

## Data Collection

We **do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data** with external servers or third parties.

## Local Storage Only

The extension only stores the following information **locally on your device**:
- Your preferred seek time setting (10 seconds or 10 minutes)

This data is stored using Chrome's built-in `storage` API and never leaves your computer.

## Permissions Explained

The extension requests the following permissions:

### `storage`
Used to save your seek time preference locally. No data is transmitted.

### `scripting`
Used to inject keyboard control functionality into the VBTV video player page.

### `activeTab`
Used to detect the currently active tab when you press keyboard shortcuts.

### Host Permissions (`https://tv.volleyballworld.com/player*`)
Allows the extension to access VBTV player pages to inject video controls.

## No External Communication

- No data is sent to external servers
- No analytics are collected
- No third-party services are used
- No advertisement tracking

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above.

## Questions

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please create an issue on our GitHub repository.
